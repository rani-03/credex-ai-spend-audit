# GTM

## Target User

The primary target users for this product are:
- Startup founders
- Small engineering teams
- Freelance developers
- AI-heavy SaaS companies
- Technical project managers

These users often subscribe to multiple AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini, and other APIs without tracking whether they are overspending or using the most cost-efficient plans.


## User Behavior Before Discovering the Tool

Before using this tool, users are likely:
- Comparing AI pricing plans
- Looking for cheaper AI alternatives
- Searching “best AI coding assistant”
- Searching “how to reduce OpenAI API cost”
- Complaining about AI subscription expenses on Reddit or X (Twitter)

The tool is useful during moments of budgeting, team scaling, or AI stack optimization.


## Online Communities

Target users frequently spend time on:
- Reddit communities like r/startups, r/ChatGPT, r/ArtificialIntelligence, r/webdev
- Indie Hackers
- Product Hunt
- Hacker News
- X (Twitter) AI founder communities
- Discord developer communities


## First 100 Users Strategy

To get the first 100 users without paid ads:
- Share the tool on Reddit startup and AI communities
- Post demo screenshots and audit results on X (Twitter)
- Launch on Product Hunt
- Share inside college developer communities and Discord servers
- Ask small startup teams to test the product
- Create short LinkedIn posts discussing AI overspending

The focus would be on creating curiosity around “How much are you overpaying for AI tools?”


## Unfair Distribution Advantage

Credex already operates in the AI infrastructure and AI credits ecosystem, which gives access to companies actively paying for expensive AI tools. This creates a natural lead generation loop where audits directly connect to potential customers interested in AI cost optimization.


## Early Success Metrics

Week 1 traction would look like:
- 100+ completed audits
- 20+ saved audit reports
- 10+ consultation requests
- Users sharing screenshots of their savings reports online

Strong engagement would indicate that users are interested in optimizing their AI software spending and discovering cheaper alternatives.
